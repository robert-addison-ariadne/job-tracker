How jobs are chosen

Every morning the tracker searches Reed around towns across the south of England the job pages of ten universities in the south, and Higherin's graduate jobs and internships, plus Remotive for remote and international roles (and ReliefWeb once it's set up). On Monday and Thursday mornings Claude also searches the wider web for good matches the feeds miss. It keeps graduate-level jobs in your areas: music (marketing & comms side), sustainability & social impact, journalism & communications, the public sector, and not-for-profits working on sustainability. Jobs whose titles use skills from your CV rank higher. Technical, senior (£50k+) and unrelated roles are left out. Jobs abroad only appear when they're a strong match, in a purple box. The summary is four key sentences taken from the full advert.
